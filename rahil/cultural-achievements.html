Participation in Play "Ba Santi"

I participated in the South East Zone cultural competition representing MANUU in the play category with our production "Ba Santi". This powerful social drama addressed important contemporary issues and showcased our theatrical talents at the zonal level.

Achievement Summary: I won 3 prizes in the South East Zone competition and also secured awards at the National level for my performance in the play "Ba Santi". Our team's dedication and hard work were recognized at both zonal and national platforms.

The play "Ba Santi" was a critical success, praised for its powerful narrative, strong performances, and social relevance. My role as the lead actor required intense preparation and emotional depth, which I delivered successfully, contributing to our team's overall success.

Mime Competition Achievement

I participated in the mime competition at the South Zone level, where our team presented a powerful silent performance that conveyed deep emotions and social messages without words. Mime requires exceptional body control, expression, and timing, which we mastered through rigorous practice.

Achievement Summary: I achieved First Position in the South Zone mime competition and successfully qualified for the National level competition. Our performance was praised for its creativity, emotional depth, and technical excellence.

The mime performance told a story of human resilience and hope, using only body language and facial expressions. The hours of practice, attention to detail, and team coordination resulted in a seamless performance that moved the audience and impressed the judges, leading to our qualification for the national competition.
