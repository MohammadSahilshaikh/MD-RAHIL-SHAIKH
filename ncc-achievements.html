Annual Training Camp (ATC) 2023

Annual Training Camp Experience

Special Adventure Camp 2024

Special Adventure Camp Participation

NCC Media Competition Winner 2024

Media Competition Victory

Participated and won first prize in the NCC State-level Media Competition 2024 in both Videography and Photography categories. The competition focused on capturing the spirit of NCC through visual storytelling.

Achievement Summary: Won First Prize in Videography category for documentary "NCC: Shaping Future Leaders" and First Prize in Photography category for photo series "Discipline in Action". Received special appreciation from the Commanding Officer for outstanding creative work.

Competition Details:

  • Videography: Created a 10-minute documentary highlighting NCC training and its impact on youth development
  • Photography: Submitted a series of 15 photographs capturing various aspects of NCC life and training
  • Themes: Discipline, Leadership, National Pride, and Social Service
  • Judges: Panel of professional photographers and media experts
  • Recognition: Featured in NCC annual magazine and awarded at state-level ceremony

Technical Skills Demonstrated:

Videography Photography Video Editing Photo Editing Storytelling Creative Direction Adobe Premiere Pro Adobe Photoshop

Impact and Recognition:

My winning entries were selected for display at the NCC headquarters and used in recruitment campaigns. The documentary was screened during the Republic Day parade preparations, reaching an audience of over 5000 cadets and officers.
